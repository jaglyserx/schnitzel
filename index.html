Rubric

Use a 1-5 score for each category so reviews stay comparable.

Schnitzel
Crust, seasoning, meat quality, texture, and portion.
Sides
Potatoes, salad, sauce, garnish, and whether they support the plate.
Atmosphere
Room, service rhythm, noise level, and the overall occasion.
Price
Value for money, not just the absolute bill size.
